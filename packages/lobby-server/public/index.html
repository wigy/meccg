Heuristic-AI uses rules to make competent decisions. Pseudo-AI lets you play both sides, making all decisions yourself. Real-AI is experimental: it plays with a trained neural network model, and its strength depends on the model you pick. MC-AI is experimental: it searches by simulating random continuations, thinks for about two seconds per move, and its strength is unproven. Modular AI is also experimental: it does not search at all, but every move it makes is one it can explain, in terms of how much the move changes its chances of winning.
