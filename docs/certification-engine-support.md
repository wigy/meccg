# Certification: engine support catalog

Which DSL effect types the engine actually supports, and the precedent set by
each card certified so far. This is the reference for **step 4** of the
`handle-certify-card` skill: check every effect on the card against the lists
below before deciding whether the engine can already play it.

This catalog lives here, and NOT in the skill prompt, on purpose. It grows by
one entry per certified card; when it lived inline in
`.claude/commands/handle-certify-card.md` it pushed that prompt past the 128KB
`MAX_ARG_STRLEN` limit on a single `execve` argument, and every certification
began failing instantly with "Argument list too long". Keep new precedent here.

See also `docs/card-effects-dsl.md` for the DSL reference itself.

## Fully implemented

- `stat-modifier` — prowess, body, direct-influence, corruption-points modifiers with value expressions, max caps, and override mechanism (`packages/shared/src/engine/effects/resolver.ts`). Direct-influence modifiers are also resolved during faction-influence-check (in `reducer.ts` and `site.ts`) and influence-check (in `organization.ts` `availableDI`). The attack-stat targets `all-attacks` (every automatic-attack and hazard creature) and `all-automatic-attacks` (site automatic-attacks only) are honoured by **both** `prowess` and `strikes` resolution (`resolveAttackProwess`/`resolveAttackStrikes` each take `isAutomaticAttack` and collect `all-automatic-attacks` for it). Used by Redoubled Force (dm-83): +2 prowess / +3 strikes to Orc/Troll automatic-attacks only. Its self-discard rides the `on-event: attack-defeated` trigger, whose context now also exposes `attack.isAutomaticAttack` (true only for a site automatic-attack / played-auto-attack, not a hazard creature) so the discard fires only when an Orc/Troll **automatic**-attack is defeated. The `faction-influence-check` context also exposes `faction.playableRegions` — the geographic regions a faction can be played in, resolved from each named `playableAt` site's `region` (plus explicit `region:` entries) via `buildFactionPlayableRegions` (`recompute-derived.ts`), added to the three faction-influence context builders (`reducer-site.ts`, `legal-actions/site.ts`, `legal-actions/pending.ts`). Used by Firiel (dm-10): "+2 direct influence against Dúnedain and factions that can be played in Anfalas, Anórien, Belfalas, Lamedon, and Lebennin" — a single direct-influence `stat-modifier` whose `$or` gate matches `influence-check`/`target.race: dunadan`, `faction.race: dunadan`, or `faction.playableRegions $includes` any of the five named regions. The `general-influence` stat is a player-level pool modifier collected in `recompute-derived.ts` from **both** a character's items / attached permanent-events (Bade to Rule le-167, Great Shadow ba-62) **and** a bare stage permanent-event in the player's `cardsInPlay` (not on any character); it raises `PlayerState.generalInfluenceBonus` (full pool, used by the defensive unused-GI subtraction). An optional `controlLimit` caps how many of the `value` points may control characters — the excess (`value - controlLimit`) is accumulated into `generalInfluenceControlPenalty`, so the **character-control budget** is `generalInfluenceControlLimit = effectiveGeneralInfluence - generalInfluenceControlPenalty` (consulted by every character-play / follower-to-GI gate: `legal-actions/organization-characters.ts`, `organization-companies.ts`, `reducer-site.ts`, `pending-reducers.ts`), while the full +value still counts for defense — mirroring the Ringwraith/Balrog +5 (CoE 1.12.R1/1.12.B1). Used by Truths of Doom (wh-108): "+6 general influence; you may only use 2 of these 6 points to control characters" (`value: 6, controlLimit: 2`, on a Pallando-specific stage permanent-event). The `all-characters` effective-stats `when` context also exposes `bearer.atOrMovingUnderDeeps` (true when the character's company's `currentSite`/`destinationSite` carries the `under-deeps` keyword — `currentSite` stays the origin for the whole M/H phase, so it covers "at", "moving from", and, via `destinationSite`, "moving to"; `companyAtOrMovingUnderDeeps` in `recompute-derived.ts`). Used by The Sun Shone Fiercely (ba-25): "-1 prowess to all Orc, Troll, Dwarf, and Ringwraith characters not at, nor moving to or from, an Under-deeps site (-2 if Doors of Night is not in play)" — a base -2 prowess `all-characters` modifier gated on `target.race $in [orc,troll,dwarf,ringwraith]` + `bearer.atOrMovingUnderDeeps $ne true`, plus a +1 softening additionally gated on `inPlay: "Doors of Night"` (net -1 with DoN, -2 without), and `duplication-limit` scope game. Site automatic-attack strike/prowess resolution also exposes the site's **effective** type as `site.siteType` in the resolver context (`reducer-site.ts` threads `effectiveSiteType` into every site auto-attack `resolveAttackStrikes` call — main, The Moon Is Dead / Incite Defenders duplicates, and Troll-purse / rescue re-faces), so an `all-automatic-attacks` modifier can gate on the site type. Used by Awaken Minions (tw-10): "The number of strikes for each automatic-attack at a Shadow-hold [{S}] or Dark-hold [{D}] site is doubled. Cannot be duplicated." — a `strikes` `op: multiply` `value: 2` `target: all-automatic-attacks` gated `when site.siteType $in [shadow-hold, dark-hold]` (multiply runs after additive modifiers, so it doubles the already-modified total) plus `duplication-limit` scope game. The `site.siteType` path is populated only for site automatic-attacks (not hazard creatures nor card-triggered/Tidings attacks), matching "automatic-attack at a … site". Direct-influence modifiers are ALSO folded into an **opponent-influence attempt** (influencing an opponent's in-play card) when they carry a `when` gated on the `opponent-influence-check` context: `reducer-site.ts` collects the influencer's `when`-gated stat-modifiers via `collectCharacterEffects` and adds them to the influencer's DI contribution. Only `when`-gated modifiers are folded there (an unconditional direct-influence modifier is already baked into effective DI / `availableDI`, so re-adding it would double-count). Used by Trifling Ring (le-346): "+3 to direct influence against characters" — two conditional direct-influence modifiers, one `when reason: influence-check` (controlling a character as a follower, via `availableDI` with a target `CharacterCard`) and one `when reason: opponent-influence-check, target.kind: character` (influencing an opponent's character); neither matches `faction-influence-check`, so the bonus is correctly excluded against factions, and `target.kind: character` excludes ally/item opponent-influence targets — plus `duplication-limit` scope `character`. Both influence contexts additionally expose stat fields for stat-gated DI bonuses: `target.mind` (omitted for avatars — `{ "target.mind": { "$gt": 0 } }` is a "has a mind" gate), `target.prowess`, and `bearer.prowess` (the influencer's **effective** prowess; effective target stats in the opponent-influence context, printed stats in `availableDI`). The condition-matcher's comparison operators (`$gt/$gte/$lt/$lte`) accept a context-path string operand resolved at match time (`resolveComparisonOperand` in `effects/condition-matcher.ts`), enabling stat-vs-stat gates. Used by Whip (le-348): "Orc or Troll only: provides +2 direct influence against one character with a mind and prowess less than the bearer's. Cannot be duplicated on a given character." — the two Trifling-Ring-style conditional DI modifiers additionally gated on `bearer.race $in [orc, troll]`, `target.mind $gt 0`, and `target.prowess $lt "bearer.prowess"`, plus `duplication-limit` scope `character` (enforced for normally-played items in `legal-actions/site.ts`).
- `check-modifier` — bonus/penalty to corruption, faction-influence checks (`resolver.ts`). For corruption-check resolutions the modifier is collected from attached hazards **and** items (`legal-actions/pending.ts`), with a context exposing `source.keywords` (the keywords on the card that enqueued the check) so items can gate on "spell", "ritual", etc. An optional `target: "company"` broadens the modifier from the bearer alone to **every character in the bearer's company** — collected once per company from items / attached permanent-events on any company member by `collectCompanyItemEffects` (`resolver.ts`, mirroring the company-scoped `stat-modifier`; the bearer's per-character item pass skips it to avoid double-counting) and folded into each member's check by `resolveCheckModifier` in both corruption subsystems (`legal-actions/pending.ts` `corruptionCheckActions` and `legal-actions/free-council.ts`). Used by I'll Be At Your Heels (le-195): a Command permanent-event on a leader — `play-target` character (`target.keywords $includes leader`) + `on-event self-enters-play` bounce of other Command cards (`move` `filter-all` `items-on-target`→`hand`, `keywords $includes Command`) + `stat-modifier direct-influence -2` (`min: 0`) + `check-modifier corruption +1 target: company` + `grant-action return-self-to-hand` (`move` self→hand). Siblings I'll Report You (le-196, +1 prowess) and Smart and Secret (le-229, +1 body) use the same shape with a `stat-modifier target: company` instead.
- `body-check-modifier` — adds `value` to the 2d6 body-check roll made against the bearer during combat (negative protects the bearer). Collected from attached items in `reducer-combat.ts` (`bodyCheckRollModifier`); applies in both hazard/automatic-attack and CvCC body checks. Used by Helm of Fear (as-126). An optional `scope: "all-attacks"` makes it **global** instead of item-bound: carried by an in-play permanent-event (either player's `cardsInPlay`), it applies to every combat body check, gated by `when` against a context exposing `attack.creatureRace` (attacking creature's normalized race) and `target.race` (body-checked character's race); collected by `globalBodyCheckRollModifier` (`combat-actions.ts`). Used by Spawn of Ungoliant (ba-24): "+1 to all body checks for Elves, Dwarves, Hobbits, Dúnedain, and Men resulting from Spider attacks." — also a `permanent-event-auto-attack` (`onDefeat: "remove-from-play"`) adding a Spawn 3×15/8 auto-attack to The Pûkel-deeps (ba-94/dm-34) and The Gem-deeps (ba-90/dm-30). An optional `scope: "bearer-combat"` instead applies to body checks arising from the **bearer's own combat**: carried by an item / attached permanent-event on a participating character, collected by `bearerCombatBodyCheckModifier` (`combat-actions.ts`) at every body-check path. The relevant bearer is the **parrying defender** for a `creature`/`attacker-character` check (a failed strike against the bearer, so the striker body-checks) and the **successful CvCC attacker** for a `character` check; the `when` context exposes `bodyCheck.target`, `bodyCheck.fromFailedStrike`, and `combat.isCvCC`. Used by Flame of Udûn (ba-58): a Demon fána permanent-event played on The Balrog (`play-target` character `target.name: "The Balrog"` + `demon-fana` keyword) — `+3 prowess` / `-2 direct influence` (`stat-modifier`), "+1 to all body checks resulting from failed strikes against The Balrog" (`bearer-combat`, `when bodyCheck.fromFailedStrike: true`) and "+1 to defending character's body check" when The Balrog attacks successfully in CvCC (`bearer-combat`, `when bodyCheck.target: character` + `combat.isCvCC: true`); the fána swap reuses `on-event self-enters-play` → move `items-on-target` (filter `demon-fana`) → hand + `grant-action return-self-to-hand`; "Discard his allies" is `on-event self-enters-play` → move **`allies-on-target`** → discard (new move zone in `reducer-move.ts`); "No other characters or allies can be in his company outside of the organization phase" is the new **`play-flag: "no-allies-in-company"`** (borne on an item, consulted by `companyHasNoAllyRestriction` in the ally-play emitter `legal-actions/site.ts`; allies are site-phase-only so no phase gate is needed, and characters only join a company during the org phase the card permits).
- `company-modifier` — applies stat modifiers to all characters in bearer's company (`resolver.ts`)
- `in-play-item-modifier` — a game-wide corruption-point / marshalling-point modifier to every in-play item matching an `itemFilter` (per-item context `{ item: { keywords, name, cardType, subtype } }`, absent → all items), sourced from a permanent-event in either player's `cardsInPlay`. Collected once per recompute by `collectInPlayItemModifiers` (`recompute-derived.ts`); `itemModifierDeltas` sums the matching `corruptionPoints`/`marshallingPoints` per item. The corruption delta is folded into each matching item's bearer corruption total in `computeEffectiveStats` (under the same Balrog-avatar exclusion as the item's printed corruption); the MP delta is added flat to the item's marshalling category in the MP tally (independent of the cross-alignment / MEWH §4 clamps on the item's printed MP). Reaches items of **both** players ("all ring items"). Used by Rumor of the One (le-224): "+1 to the corruption points and the marshalling points for all ring items. Discard when any play deck is exhausted. Cannot be duplicated." — paired with `on-event: play-deck-exhausted` self-discard `move` (the Tokens to Show as-101 / Safe from the Shadow as-54 pattern, fired by `completeDeckExhaust`) and `duplication-limit` scope `game`. Also used by Scorba at Home (td-65): "each major item gives an additional corruption point." — `itemFilter` `{ "item.subtype": "major" }` (the `subtype` context path was added for it), `corruptionPoints: 1`, no MP delta. And by Itangast at Home (td-38): "each greater item gives an additional corruption point" — `itemFilter` `{ "item.subtype": "greater" }`, `corruptionPoints: 1`, paired with its `dragon-at-home` Gold Hill augmentation (below).
- `dragon-at-home` — augments a Dragon's lair (every site version whose `lairOf` matches the carrying permanent-event's `manifestId`) with an additional Dragon automatic-attack while the At-Home is in play, suppressed while the same Dragon's Ahunt long-event is also in play; a defeated manifestation chain instead strips the lair's Dragon attacks entirely. Implemented in `engine/manifestations.ts` (`getActiveAutoAttacks` / `collectAtHomeAttacks` / `isAhuntInPlay`). Augment attacks are modeled strikes+prowess only (no `body`), matching the codebase convention for printed Dragon-lair automatic-attacks. Used by Daelomin at Home (td-11, + `discard-for-hazard-limit`) and Scorba at Home (td-65): Dragon 3×13 on Zarak Dûm — both site versions (hero td-181, minion le-417) carry `lairOf: "td-63"` (the minion tag was added with td-65's certification). Also by Itangast at Home (td-38): Dragon 3×19 on Gold Hill (hero td-176, `lairOf: "td-36"`), suppressed by Itangast Ahunt (td-37) via shared `manifestId` td-36; td-38 additionally carries the greater-item `in-play-item-modifier` above.
- `corruption-source-multiplier` — a game-wide effect (carried by a permanent/long event in either player's `cardsInPlay`) that scales **one** of every character's corruption sources by `multiplier` (default 2 — "doubled"). Collected once per recompute by `collectCorruptionSourceMultipliers` (`recompute-derived.ts`), one multiplier per in-play copy. In `computeEffectiveStats` each character's individual corruption sources are tracked (only while a copy is in play and the bearer isn't the Balrog avatar, whose borne items contribute no corruption): each corruption-bearing item's total CP (printed + `in-play-item-modifier` delta) is one source, each attached `hazard-corruption` card is one source, and each card contributing corruption via a `stat-modifier` on `corruption-points` (grouped by source card instance — e.g. The One Ring, Durin's Axe on a Dwarf) is one source. `corruptionSourceMultiplierDelta` adds the extra corruption: the controlling player minimises, so each copy scales the character's smallest remaining source (largest multiplier paired with smallest source, per the rearrangement inequality). No source → no effect; reaches characters of **both** players ("each character"). Used by The Balance of Things (tw-93): "Each character has the corruption points doubled for one of his sources of corruption (the player controlling the character chooses)." — "Unique" is deck-level (1 copy/deck), so no in-play `duplication-limit` is declared.
- `duplication-limit` — enforced for scopes "game", "player", "site", "character", and "company" (the company scope covers both allies and items, in `legal-actions/site.ts`) plus combat scopes "turn"/"attack". The "character" scope covers **two** cases: a card that *attaches* to the character (rings/permanent events — copies counted via the bearer's `items`, `legal-actions/site.ts`), and a *non-attaching* filter-target short event that leaves a character-targeted active constraint on play (counted by matching `sourceDefinitionId` + `target.kind: "character"` + `target.characterId` in the org-phase filter-target emitter, `legal-actions/organization.ts`). Used by Words of Menace and Deceit (le-258): its rest-of-turn +5 `character-stat-modifier` marks the target, so a second copy is unplayable on that same character but still playable on a different eligible one.
- `character-stat-modifier` (short-event, no gate) — the `on-event: self-enters-play` → `add-constraint character-stat-modifier` path (`reducer-events.ts`) applies a turn-scoped stat bonus to `action.targetCharacterId`, the character chosen by a `play-target: character` filter. Used unconditionally (no `requiresCardInPlay`) by Words of Menace and Deceit (le-258): "+5 to the character's direct influence for the rest of the turn" — a `direct-influence` +5 folded into the target's `effectiveStats.directInfluence` (and thus `availableDI`) for the turn. Companion `enqueue-corruption-check modifier -4` gated `when $not target.race ringwraith` ("Unless he is a Ringwraith, he makes a corruption check modified by -4"), plus `duplication-limit` scope character. Filter is `target.skills $includes "spirit-magic"`, so a Ringwraith without spirit-magic (Dwar — sorcery) is not a legal target.
- `name-alias` — while the bearer is in `cardsInPlay`, its `as` name is added to the in-play names list (`inPlayNamesOf` in `recompute-derived.ts`, which backs `buildInPlayNames` and `buildControllerInPlayNames`), so every DSL `when: { inPlay: "<alias>" }` gate is satisfied. The card's own name and `duplication-limit` counts (`countCopiesInPlay`, by definition name) are unaffected, so an aliasing card never collides with the real aliased card. Used by Skies of Fire (le-228), which "acts as Gates of Morning for the purposes of interpreting hazards". The few inline in-play-name builders (`legal-actions/combat.ts`, `legal-actions/movement-hazard.ts`) were switched to `buildInPlayNames` so the alias is honored on every hazard-interpretation path.
- `environment-override` — while the bearer is in `cardsInPlay`, reshapes game-wide which named environments are considered in/out of play: `considerInPlay` names are treated as in play (with no actual card) and `considerNotInPlay` names as out of play (even while their card stays in `cardsInPlay`). `collectEnvironmentOverride` (`reducer-utils.ts`) is consulted by `buildInPlayNames` (the `inPlay` condition context), `inPlayNamesForPlayerDeep` (combat `defender.inPlay`), and the name-in-play predicates behind `card-in-play`/`card-not-in-play` play-conditions (`isCardNameInPlayOrCharacters`, `isCardNameInPlayForPlayer`) — so every "is X in play?" query stays consistent. Removals precede additions. Only the named environments are touched (not `countCopiesInPlay`), so playing an actual copy is never blocked. Used by Peril Returned (td-54): Doors of Night considered in, Gates of Morning considered out (the Gates of Morning card remains removable normally).
- `region-keying-boost` — turn-scoped environment that softens creature keying: one region of type `from` in a company's site path counts as `count` regions of type `asType` (boosts are alternatives, at most one applied per keying check, never combined). On play the short-event adds a `region-keying-boost` active constraint; the creature-keying matchers (`findCreatureKeyingMatches`, `checkCreatureKeying`) consult it via `engine/region-keying.ts` (`collectRegionKeyingBoosts`, `regionPathsWithBoosts`). Used by Withered Lands (td-85), gated on Doors of Night via a `play-condition`/`site-path`/`inPlay` clause.
- `draw-modifier` — adjusts the resource/hazard draw counts at the movement/hazard draw step (`draw: 'resource' | 'hazard'`, numeric or expression `value`, optional `min` floor). Resolved in `mh-steps.ts` `transitionToDrawCards` via `resolveDrawModifier`. Effects are collected from **both** the moving company's characters (`collectCharacterEffects`) **and** the active (moving) player's own in-play events/environments in `cardsInPlay` (`collectPlayerInPlayEffects`), so a resource long-event not carried by any character can contribute; collecting only from the active player's cardsInPlay keeps the bonus from ever helping the opponent. The value-expression / `when` context exposes `sitePath` counts (`wildernessCount`, …, plus `regionCount` = total path length) and the top-level `movementType` (`starter`/`region`/`special`/`under-deeps`). Used by Radagast/Alatar (character-carried) and A Short Rest (td-95): a Long-event granting `"4 - sitePath.regionCount"` extra resource draws to each moving company, gated on an actual region site path (`movementType` in region/starter, `regionCount` 1..3) so under-deeps/special movement and 4+-region paths grant nothing and the bonus is never negative (CRF 22 ruling).
- `absorb-wound` — prevents wound from a successful strike; attacker rolls to potentially discard the item (`packages/shared/src/engine/reducer-combat.ts` `resolveStrike` + `handleShieldDiscardRoll`)
- `face-strike-on-tap` — activated ability on an in-play item (or character-attached permanent event) that lets its bearer face one of an attack's strikes **regardless of the attack's normal capabilities and the bearer's status**. During the `assign-strikes` defender phase, while the item is untapped, its bearer is in the defending company, and an unassigned strike remains, the defending player may tap it (the `face-strike-on-tap` action, `legal-actions/combat.ts` `assignStrikeActions` defender branch → `handleFaceStrikeOnTap` in `combat-actions.ts`) to add a strike-facing to the bearer even while he is tapped/wounded (ordinary status gate bypassed). The assignment is flagged `StrikeAssignment.reduceAttackBodyOnParry`; when the bearer parries that strike (`characterTotal >= creature prowess`, the strike fails to wound him), `combat.creatureBody` is reduced by `bodyReductionOnParry` for the rest of the combat (applied in `combat-strike.ts` `resolveStrikeCore`, including to that strike's own creature body check). Used by Bow of Alatar (wh-90): placed on Alatar (`play-target` character filter `{ target.name: "Alatar" }`), tap to face a strike and reduce the attack's body by 1 on a parry; also carries `stage-points` 2.
- `stat-modifier` prowess `when: { "combat.strikeMode": "tap" }` — a prowess modifier that applies only when the character **taps to face a strike**. `computeCombatProwess` (`recompute-derived.ts`) takes the strike-resolution `strikeMode` (`'tap'`/`'untap'`/`'dodge'`/`'reroll'`) and exposes it as `combat.strikeMode`; the creature-facing path (`combat-strike.ts` `resolveStrikeCore`) passes the actual mode, and the tap/untap need shown by `legal-actions/combat.ts` `resolveStrikeActions` is computed per-mode (`baseProwessTap`/`baseProwessUntap`). Because `combat.strikeMode` is absent outside strike resolution, the modifier never leaks into effective-stats. Used by Stabbing Tongue of Fire (ba-81) and Whip of Many Thongs (ba-82): "+1 prowess when tapping to face a strike".
- `combat-cancel-weapon` — an in-play item ability usable only during a company-vs-company combat (CvCC) in which the item's bearer's company is a participant. The controller pays `cost` (`{ tap: "self" }`) and picks one `weapon`-keyword item borne by a character in the *opposing* company; its instance is appended to `CombatState.suppressedWeaponInstanceIds` (`cancel-weapon-effects` action, `cancelWeaponActions` in `legal-actions/combat.ts` → `handleCancelWeaponEffects` in `combat-cancel.ts`, wired through `COMBAT_HANDLERS`). While suppressed, `collectCharacterEffects` (`effects/resolver.ts`) drops every effect the weapon sources and `computeEffectiveStats` (`recompute-derived.ts`) skips its structural `prowessModifier`/`bodyModifier`, so the weapon contributes nothing to its bearer's combat stats (CvCC reads `effectiveStats.prowess`); the weapon is **not** discarded and the suppression clears when combat finalizes (the list lives on the combat state). A weapon just declared onto a character during the current chain is suppressed identically (contribution is always read live from the item), backing "(even declared in the same chain of effects)". Offered only to the item's controller, whichever side The Balrog is on; the scan deliberately looks at items on the Balrog avatar (the Balrog-specific Whip is exempt from the general "items on the Balrog have no effect" ban, unlike `modifyAttackActions`). Used by Whip of Many Thongs (ba-82): "If The Balrog is in company vs. company combat, tap this item to cancel all effects of one weapon of your choice (even declared in the same chain of effects) in an opponent's company until the end of the combat. This does not discard the weapon." — plus the shared `item-play-site` (non-Darkhaven Under-deeps, `allowTapped`), `play-target` `{ target.name: "The Balrog" }`, and the strike-mode prowess gate above.
- `join-combat-force-strike` — a resource short-event played by the defending player in the pre-assignment window of the `assign-strikes` sub-phase (same window as `company-combat-boost`: `combat.phase === 'assign-strikes'`, no strikes assigned, `playerId === defendingPlayerId`). It brings a named character (`characterName`) into the defending company if absent — "considered movement with no movement/hazard phase", so only the company membership arrays change and the character is **not** restored after combat (unlike an Alatar haven-jump) — adds it to `CombatState.forcedStrikeTargets` (the defender-assignment status gate is now bypassed for a forced target so it must face a strike "regardless of any conflicting effects" even while tapped/wounded), and, when `tapAfterAttack`, schedules a `PostAttackEffect` `tapIfUntapped` (the same Alatar "following the attack, tap" mechanism). Playability gates: `requiresSiteKeyword` (the defending company's `currentSite` or `destinationSite` def carries the keyword, e.g. `under-deeps`) and `notInPlay` (the named card must not be in `buildInPlayNames`). Offering `joinCombatForceStrikeActions` + the forced-target bypass in `assignStrikeActions` (`legal-actions/combat.ts`); reducer block in `reducer-events.ts` `handlePlayResourceShortEvent`. Used by Vanguard of Might (ba-79): "Playable if a company at or moving to an Under-deeps site is facing an attack and Flame of Udûn is not in play. If not in the company, The Balrog immediately joins the company. … The Balrog must face a strike from the attack, regardless of any conflicting effects. Following the attack, if untapped, tap The Balrog." (`characterName: "The Balrog"`, `tapAfterAttack`, `requiresSiteKeyword: "under-deeps"`, `notInPlay: "Flame of Udûn"`; "Balrog specific" is a deck-construction keyword, no play-time gate).
- `combat-discard-opponent-item` — a Balrog resource short-event played during a company-vs-company combat (CvCC) in which The Balrog is untapped and a participant on the acting player's side; on play the card-player chooses one item borne by the *opposing* company and discards it (to the opponent's discard pile). Field-less: the opposing company and Balrog gate are read from the live combat state. Offered by `combatDiscardOpponentItemActions` (`legal-actions/combat.ts`, wired into the CvCC windows of `combatActions` alongside the Whip's `cancel-weapon`) as one `play-short-event` per eligible hand card when the acting player owns one of the two CvCC companies with The Balrog untapped in it, the opposing company bears ≥1 genuine item, the `card-in-play` play-condition (Flame of Udûn) holds, and the turn-scoped `duplication-limit` is not reached (`countConstraintsFromDefinition`); offered to whichever side the Balrog is on (mirrors `cancelWeaponActions`).
